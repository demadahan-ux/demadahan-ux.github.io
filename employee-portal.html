THE GOAL

Bring the daily
operating picture together.

I built this employee dashboard using Google Apps Script, with connected Google Sheets supporting the data workflow. Upload and download capabilities support working with the underlying records.

The portal gives users a common place to move between employee information, scorecards, attendance, announcements, and leave data. The focus is practical: make the relevant operating view easier to find and review.

THE MAIN VIEWS

One portal.
Several operating views.

01 / OVERVIEW

Dashboard & adherence

Employee totals and support-performance summaries, plus recent announcements, loss hours, and overbreak and overlunch indicators.

02 / PERFORMANCE

Scorecards & record review

Current scorecards, previous records, and analytics navigation. Filters include month, team, status, top-five selection, and agent search.

03 / PEOPLE

Employee record management

A searchable employee list with identifiers, contact details, team, role, status, and hire dates, plus add and update controls.

04 / PLANNING

Leave requests & calendar

A leave-data area with request-management and calendar views, including month and year selection for reviewing scheduled leave.

THE CONNECTED WORKFLOW

Google Sheets data.
A purpose-built interface.

The original application connects to Google Sheets through Google Apps Script. Its upload and download workflows connect spreadsheet work with the portal’s operating views.
