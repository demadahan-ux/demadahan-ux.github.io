03 / SUPPORT IN PRACTICE

Thoughtful words.

Explore illustrative examples of customer communication, escalation documentation, and team coaching.

THE CONVERSATION

When a customer
feels unheard.

“I’ve contacted support twice, and I still don’t know what’s happening. I’m tired of explaining this.”
THE GOAL

Acknowledge the frustration and make the next step clear.

↗Example replyEMAIL / CHAT

Hi Alex,

I’m sorry you’ve had to follow up more than once without a clear update. I understand why that’s frustrating.

I’ll review the earlier conversation and what’s already been checked so you don’t have to start from the beginning. Then I’ll explain the next step and when you can expect an update.

Could you share your ticket number so I can find the right conversation?

Demi

✓ Empathy · Ownership · One clear request
THE HANDOVER

Give the next person
the full picture.

A customer reports a subscription charge after requesting cancellation and needs a billing review.
THE GOAL

Help the next team act without making the customer repeat the story.

↗Example case noteINTERNAL
Customer’s concern
Reports a charge after a cancellation request; asks for review and confirmation of subscription status.
Checks to complete
Confirm account ownership through the approved process, then check the cancellation date, billing timeline, and earlier notes.
Requested action
Ask the billing team to verify the charge and confirm the resolution available under the applicable policy.
Customer update
Explain who is reviewing the case and confirm the next update time once agreed. Record the follow-up owner.
✓ Context · Required action · Clear ownership
THE COACHING MOMENT

Make good support
more consistent.

An agent gives an accurate answer, but the customer leaves unsure about what happens next.
THE GOAL

Turn specific feedback into a small, repeatable improvement.

↗Example coaching noteTEAM SUPPORT
Recognise what worked
“You explained the policy clearly and kept your tone professional throughout the conversation.”
Focus on one improvement
“Before closing, explain the next action, who will take it, and when the customer should expect an update.”
Practise together
Rewrite the closing response together. Ask the agent to explain how the customer will know what to do next.
Follow up
Review a later conversation for clarity and recognise progress in the next coaching discussion.
✓ Specific feedback · Practice · Follow-up
